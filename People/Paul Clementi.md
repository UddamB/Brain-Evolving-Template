# Paul Clementi

Mentor/advisor who ran a marketing, business strategy, and personal finance session for Uddam; also runs a Melaleuca side business generating residual income from consumables (~$120k earned to date, 97% monthly customer retention). Advocates leverage-based marketing — replacing inefficient 1-on-1 meetings with group presentations (free library "TED Talks," bi-weekly library networking events, trade association guest speaking) to reach more prospects per hour. Core principle: build relationships before you need them ("dig your well before you're thirsty"). Uses FileMaker Pro for CRM and MailChimp to track newsletter engagement; recommends *The 12 Week Year* for goal-setting.
