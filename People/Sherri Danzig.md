# Sherri Danzig

Owner of Choosing Vibrancy, a 28-year wellness business spanning wellness consulting (sleep tech, water/air filtration), self-care coaching for women, and a 36-year massage therapy practice. Already highly automated — a team in Mexico manages operations, a dedicated person handles social media, and she uses AI personally (e.g. for newsletters). Deliberately avoids templated approaches, favoring personal one-on-one coaching; client acquisition is networking-based (Alignable, in-person, global organizations).
