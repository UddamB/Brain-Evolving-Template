## Topics

### Client Acquisition & Onboarding
- Primary channel: NASDAQ Mentor Makers — free mentorship builds trust and screens for paid clients (~1 in 4 mentees converts); lets both sides assess fit before a financial commitment.
- Onboarding is organic and relationship-driven, not a formal corporate intake; builds relationships that lead to paid work or referral partnerships.

### Advisory Philosophy & Value
- Core function: a "co-pilot" who helps founders identify risks/opportunities they're unaware of — founders often focus only on the P&L top line and miss admin costs/liabilities.
- Example — S-Corp advice: when a bookkeeper suggests becoming an S-Corp, Chad prompts the founder to ask "What's in it for the bookkeeper?" (more payroll/filings = more billable hours for the bookkeeper), ensuring the decision serves the founder, not the vendor.
- Example — accrual accounting trap: a corporation earned significant 2025 revenue but hadn't collected it all by year-end; under GAAP/accrual accounting this created a surprise $30k-per-partner tax bill on earned (not collected) revenue, forcing IRS negotiation or a loan. Chad highlights this pitfall and pushes for proactive Annual Operating Plans.
- Chad advises and refers out to specialists (CPAs, lawyers) rather than performing the work himself.

### Alignable & Social Media Strategy
- Alignable: primarily for finding referral partners (bookkeepers, CPAs); secondary use is attending low-attendance educational events for high-quality networking — has produced two paid clients via entrepreneurship-focused Smart Connects.
- LinkedIn: consistent engagement (liking, commenting on others' posts) beats chasing virality — builds an audience and feedback loop that raises visibility for everyone involved.
