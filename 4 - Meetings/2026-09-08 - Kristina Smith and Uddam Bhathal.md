### Problem: Manual Outreach & Underutilized Tools
- ~1,000 clients from 18 years in business; primary challenge is lack of systematic engagement.
- Manual outreach: 3–4 hrs/week via calls and physical "tickle file" system.
- Underutilized: Sprinklr (company-provided, unused), Reminder Media (2,000 emails/mo, underutilized), Redtail (not active).

### Solution: 3-Part Email Marketing System
1. Unified client roster: consolidate Sprinklr + Reminder Media + Medicare files; tag households by financial gap.
2. Gap-based drip campaigns via Sprinklr and Reminder Media (automated sequences per financial gap).
3. Automated reply sorting: "Now" (calendar booking), "Later" (follow-up sequence), "Not Now."

### ROI & Investment
- Inputs: 1,000 clients, 60% with insurance-only gaps, 3% reply rate, 25% wealth conversion.
- Output: 12 reviews + 3 new wealth households/year; 156 hrs saved.
- Bundle: $1,200 setup + $450/mo; individual: $200–$750/service.
