## Topics

### Problem: Ineffective Sales Language
- Uddam's initial pitch — "I help people save time with AI" — was too generic to differentiate him from competitors.
- Brian noted the need for a structured sales *system*: "systems are more profitable than skills."

### Solution: The 3-Part Story Framework
- Replaces pitching with authentic storytelling (Brian demonstrated this live by reframing Uddam's service in ~30 seconds).
- **Part 1 — Problem ("You know how...")**: introduce a common, easy-to-agree-on problem; use "they/them" to avoid sounding accusatory; goal is to get any form of agreement (even a nod) before continuing.
- **Part 2 — Solution ("Well, what I do is...")**: state your role as the solution, briefly — don't explain the process.
- **Part 3 — Benefit ("So that they can...")**: the most important part — explicitly state the outcome/benefit so it attaches to your name and avoids commoditization.

### Mindset Shift: "Lucky Them" & "Fit Before Fee"
- "Lucky them": project genuine confidence that clients are better off for having hired you.
- "Fit before fee": prioritize working with people who are a good personality/communication fit — easier communication makes the work more effective and enjoyable.

### Application & Offer
- Uddam ran the framework in a live role-play and it felt authentic.
- Brian offered a "fit before fee" trial: "Selling Uddam and his services bootcamp" — 2-hour 1-on-1 session + 30 days of on-call coaching for $997. Uddam declined, citing lack of time.
