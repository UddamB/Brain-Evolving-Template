## Topics

### Uddam's Business & Marketing Challenge
- Uddam's company builds AI voice agents for after-hours calls, primarily for dental clinics, med spas, and financial firms.
- Current marketing (email, Alignable, word of mouth) is 1-on-1 and doesn't scale.

### Leverage-Based Marketing
- Core principle: become a memorable resource now so prospects invite you in to bid later, rather than chasing an immediate sale.
- **Library "TED Talks":** a free, 20-minute community-service talk (e.g. AI for businesses) — libraries provide the room and list the event on their calendar, drawing 5–15 attendees; positions Uddam as a local expert and scales better than 1-on-1 meetings.
- **Library networking events:** host a free, bi-weekly event for 8–10 local businesses (3-min pitches all round, one 10-min deep dive, then social networking) to become the most memorable person in the room.
- **Trade association guest speaking:** speak at dental/medical association meetings to reach 30+ targeted prospects at once.

### Long-Term Financial Strategy
- Paul's Melaleuca side business generates residual income from consumables — $120k earned to date, 97% monthly customer retention; motivated by building a safety net for a disabled son.
- **Rule of 72:** divide 72 by the annual interest rate for years to double an investment — $10k at 10% becomes $20k in 7.2 years, ~$160k in ~29 years; start early and take more risk while young.
- Paul's own portfolio splits between ~10%-return stocks (managed by a financial planner) and ~4%-return rolling CDs maturing every 3 months for steady income.

### Productivity & Systems
- **Goal-setting:** *The 12 Week Year* — compress annual goals into 3-month cycles, focusing on the top 3 priorities each week to fight procrastination.
- **Paul's systems:** FileMaker Pro for client profiles/follow-ups, MailChimp to track newsletter opens/clicks and identify engaged prospects, and AI to outline meeting recordings for newsletter content.
- **Tax deductions:** home office (percentage of home expenses if used exclusively for business) and mileage (e.g. 42 cents/mile) — consult an accountant to ensure compliance.
