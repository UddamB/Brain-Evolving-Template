### Client Acquisition & Needs Analysis
- Pre-appointment vetting call assesses a prospect's fit and financial situation before booking a full meeting.
- DIME method quantifies required coverage: **D**ebt + **I**ncome (×10–15 for dependents) + **M**ortgage/rent + **E**ducation expenses.
- All contacts — clients, prospects, trainers — are tracked in a single Excel spreadsheet spanning every state Nathan is licensed in (MD, VA, TX, NH).

### Retirement Strategy & Annuities
- Core problem framing: retirees fear outliving their money due to rising costs, inflation, and uncertain Social Security.
- Annuities act as a "personal pension," complementing volatile 401(k)s with a guaranteed income stream.
- Product types: **Fixed** (guaranteed steady growth) vs. **Indexed** (tied to a market index like the S&P 500, with a 0% floor and a cap, e.g., 8.25% — the insurer keeps the spread above the cap in exchange for downside protection).
- Nathan works with multiple A-rated carriers (Athene, Antico, F&G) to match the product to each client's goals.

### Athene Performance Elite 10 — Worked Example
- 14-year contract (age 55 → 70) starting from a $300,000 rollover.
- $36,000 premium bonus added on day one → $336,000 starting contract value.
- Projected to grow past $800,000 by year 14 under the 0% floor / 8.25% cap, based on historical S&P 500 performance.
- Death benefit matches the cumulative contract value; base fees are under 3%, with riders (e.g., long-term care) priced separately.

### Client Retention & Bottleneck
- Retention strategy: personal check-ins for annual reviews and birthdays to confirm plans still fit client needs.
- Primary bottleneck: client follow-up consumes the most time.
- Goal: automate follow-ups to free time for new client acquisition and financial education content (e.g., a planned newsletter article on starting retirement coverage earlier).
