- Program goal: generate qualified leads for Stillwater's core business — building and maintaining custom AI assistants.

### Methodology: "Still Prompt" Framework
- **Situation** — provide context (e.g., OCI funding, June 19 deadline).
- **Task** — define the goal (e.g., identify top 3 administrative benefits for the vertical).
- **Identity** — assign the AI a persona (e.g., "world-class expert on agri-foods").
- **Learning** — instruct the AI to ask clarifying questions to refine its output.
- Uploading the existing construction program document as grounding material improves output quality — it's the template for the new verticals.
